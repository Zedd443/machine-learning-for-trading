# Kurikulum Trading 24 Minggu

Target: membangun satu strategi yang jelas, dapat diuji, dan punya statistik historis/forward-test yang cukup untuk dinilai.

## Fase 1 — Survival dan market mechanics (Minggu 1–4)
M1 Risk & R-multiple: sizing, stop distance, R, drawdown. Lulus jika 20/20 sizing benar.
M2 Orders & execution: market/limit/stop, spread, slippage, maker/taker, liquidation/mark price.
M3 Structure: swing, HH/HL/LH/LL, range, breakout, failed breakout. Markup 50 chart.
M4 Volatility: ATR, expansion/contraction, volatility regime.

## Fase 2 — Reading the market (Minggu 5–8)
M5 Momentum: continuation, acceleration/deceleration; jangan generalisasi paper saham ke BTC 15m.
M6 Mean reversion: deviation, failed extension, range reversion, regime dependency.
M7 Liquidity & microstructure: bid/ask, spread, depth, stop clusters sebagai hipotesis.
M8 Derivatives context: volume, open interest, funding, basis, liquidation.

## Fase 3 — Statistik minimum trader (Minggu 9–12)
M9 Probability: conditional probability, base rate, expected value.
M10 Metrics: win rate, avg win/loss, expectancy, PF, max DD, losing streak.
M11 Sampling: variance, sample size, selection bias.
M12 Backtest failure modes: look-ahead, data snooping, survivorship, overfitting, costs.

## Fase 4 — Bangun satu setup (Minggu 13–16)
M13 Hypothesis: “Dalam kondisi X, setelah Y, entry Z punya expectancy positif setelah biaya.”
M14 Operational definition: larang kata kabur seperti kuat/clean/valid tanpa kriteria.
M15 Pilot 30–50 trade untuk debugging rule.
M16 Freeze rule v1.

## Fase 5 — Evidence (Minggu 17–20)
M17–18 Backtest utama: target 100–200 occurrence jika data memungkinkan.
M19 Segmentasi: long/short, session, volatility regime, trend/range, BTC/ETH.
M20 Robustness: biaya lebih tinggi, parameter digeser sedikit, periode berbeda.

## Fase 6 — Forward test & execution (Minggu 21–24)
M21–22 Paper trade/size sangat kecil, fokus adherence.
M23 Bandingkan backtest vs forward-test.
M24 Retain, revise, atau reject berdasarkan evidence.

## Ritme pekerja sampai 18:00
Senin–Jumat: 30–45 menit satu objective kecil.
Sabtu: 60–90 menit backtest/review.
Minggu: 30–45 menit planning + competency test.

## Definisi kemajuan
Beginner: risk + structure konsisten.
Intermediate: ide bisa diubah menjadi rule + statistik.
Advanced: robustness, regime, execution.
Mature: keputusan berbasis process + evidence, bukan outcome satu trade.
